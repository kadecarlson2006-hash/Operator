| 50 | A declined decision discards the model's own confidence and relevance | UNKNOWN TO VERIFY | `ModelDecisionEngine.parse` turns any `shouldSpeak: false` into `ResponseDecision.silence(...)`, which hardcodes confidence 1.0 and relevance 0.0; only the reason code survives. It costs nothing today because the floors are not consulted when the model declines - but it destroys the one diagnostic needed to tune them, since "how close did it come to speaking" is exactly what is thrown away. | 51 | Decision reason codes are model-authored free text, not a fixed vocabulary | UNKNOWN TO VERIFY | The same scenarios produced different codes across two runs minutes apart: the invoice case gave NO_VERIFIED_INFORMATION then NO_CONFIRMED_FACT, and the unanswered question gave NOTHING_WORTH_SAYING then NO_DIRECT_REQUEST. `parse` takes whatever string the model supplies. | 59 | The voice gate cannot tell the user's speech from anyone else's, including a television | PARTIALLY VERIFIED | `VoiceActivityDetector` measures energy against a learned noise floor; it has no speaker discrimination, and the transcription provider returns text without diarisation (which is why rolling transcript lines are attributed to "Someone"). Steady noise - a fan, traffic, a low murmur - should be absorbed by calibration. **Speech-shaped noise should not be**: a television playing dialogue is real speech at speech energy, so the gate will very likely open and Operator will transcribe the TV. No threshold fixes that, because nothing is wrong with the threshold. The consequence is a privacy one and belongs on the record: in a room with a television, or with other people talking, ambient speech is uploaded and transcribed. **Measured 2026-09-07, and it is the finding that matters most so far: 8 utterances during repeated attempts at a silent minute in an ordinary living room.** Five attempts were abandoned because a baby elsewhere in the room kept making noise. Every one of the 8 cleared both the energy threshold and the 350 ms minimum, so none were transients, and every one would have been uploaded and transcribed had the backend been reachable. This is correct behaviour for a voice-activity detector - it hears energy, not identity - and it is exactly why no threshold change addresses it. Measure both cases separately - steady noise with the TV muted, then the same room with dialogue playing - before treating always-on listening as usable anywhere but a quiet room. Options if it lands badly: a wake word, push-to-talk as the default rather than always-on, or speaker enrolment; each is a real piece of work and none is built. |
